# The long decline of global hunger

## A steep fall
The UN FAO estimated that one-in-four people in developing countries — about
33% — suffered from hunger in 1970. Rates then fell sharply, reaching 12% by
2015, a decline of 31 percentage points. In other words, the share of people
going hungry more than doubled over those decades.

## Where we are now
Progress has not been even. Globally, nearly one-in-ten people still do not get
enough food to eat, whereas in North America and Europe the rate is below 2.5%.
