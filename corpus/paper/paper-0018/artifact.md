# Alcohol around the world

## A common disorder
Globally, around 1% of people — about one in ten — have an alcohol use
disorder, meaning about 99% do not.

## Alcohol and road deaths
In South Africa and Papua New Guinea, more than half of all traffic deaths are
attributable to alcohol — leaving more than half from all other causes combined.

## A historical peak
In France in the 1920s, the average adult drank about 22.1 litres of pure alcohol
a year, which works out to roughly 600 millilitres a day.
