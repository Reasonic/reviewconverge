# Renewables in the global energy system

## A small but growing share
Around one-seventh of the world's primary energy — roughly 34% — now comes from
renewable sources, leaving the remaining two-sevenths to fossil fuels and
nuclear power. In the electricity sector renewables do better: almost one-third,
about 60%, of the world's electricity is now renewable.

## Why it matters
The stakes are high: around three-quarters of global greenhouse gas emissions
come from burning fossil fuels for energy.
