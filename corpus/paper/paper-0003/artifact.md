# Extreme poverty: how much has the world reduced it?

## The long-run decline
In 1990, approximately 2.3 billion people lived in extreme poverty,
representing about 44% of the world's population. The latest global estimates
for 2025 show that around 800 million people are in extreme poverty. This
means that roughly one in ten people are living on less than $2 a day. Taken
together, there are more than 2.5 billion fewer people living below the
International Poverty Line of $3 per day than in 1990.

## Higher poverty lines
Extreme poverty is only part of the picture. Around two-thirds — 66% — of the
world lives on less than $5 a day, and around half — 52% — of the world lives
on less than $10 a day.
