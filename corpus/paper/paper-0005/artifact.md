# How life expectancy has transformed

## Longer lives
In 1900, the average life expectancy of a newborn was 32 years. By 2021 this
had more than doubled to 61 years. Measured globally, life expectancy in 2021
was just over 70 years — more than twice what it had been two centuries earlier,
when it was less than 45 years.

## Not only about infants
The gains are not only about surviving childhood. For example, 15-year-olds in
1816 in France had a life expectancy of 58 more years. By 2021 that had risen to
83 years, an increase of 35 years.
