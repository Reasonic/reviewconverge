# Monthly Budget Review: Summary for Fiscal Year 2025

## Overview
The federal budget deficit totaled $1.8 trillion in fiscal year 2025, a
decrease of $41 billion (or 6 percent) from the shortfall recorded in fiscal
year 2024.

## Receipts and outlays
Revenues rose by 6 percent, or $317 billion, driven by higher collections of
individual income taxes and customs duties, partly offset by a decline in
corporate tax receipts. Outlays grew by 4 percent, or $375 billion, with
increases in the largest benefit programs and in net interest on the public
debt, which surpassed $1 trillion for the first time. Because the increase in
outlays was smaller than the increase in revenues, the deficit edged down
relative to the previous year.

## Relative to the economy
Measured against the size of the economy, the 2025 deficit equaled 5.9 percent
of gross domestic product (GDP), down from 6.3 percent in 2024, a decline of
0.7 percentage points. Federal debt held by the public rose to 96.8 percent of
GDP at the end of 2025, up from 97.4 percent a year earlier.
