# Who pays for healthcare

## The rise of public spending
Government health spending was once tiny. In 1880 it was below 1% of GDP in
every high-income country; by 1970 it had risen above 2% of GDP — a more than
tenfold rise in under a century.

## A concentrated cost
Health spending is highly concentrated. In the United States in 2009, the top
5% of spenders accounted for almost half of all health spending, leaving the
other 95% to account for just one-tenth. The top 1% alone accounted for more
than 60% of the total.
