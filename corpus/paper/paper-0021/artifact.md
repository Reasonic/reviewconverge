# The plastic problem

## A surge in production
In 1950 the world produced just two million tonnes of plastic. It now produces
over 450 million tonnes a year — more than a 20-fold increase.

## Where it goes
The world generates around 350 million tonnes of plastic waste each year, of
which around one-quarter — roughly 250 million tonnes — is mismanaged. Between 1
and 2 million tonnes end up in the ocean, meaning only about 5% of plastic
waste reaches the sea.
