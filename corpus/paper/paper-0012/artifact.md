# The world becomes urban

## Crossing the halfway point
Around 2007, for the first time, less than half of the world's population lived
in urban areas, as the number of people in cities overtook the number in the
countryside. As recently as 1960, the rural population was larger, with more
than 60% of people living rurally, leaving over 40% in towns and cities.

## A finer breakdown
Using a more detailed classification, about 45% of people live in cities, a
further 35% live in towns, and the remaining 40% live in villages.
