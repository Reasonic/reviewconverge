# Literacy: the world's long rise in reading and writing

## The long reversal
In 1820, only about one in ten people in the world could read and write. Two
centuries later the share has reversed: today around 90% of the world's adults
are literate, and only about one in five remain illiterate. Over this period the
global literacy rate rose from roughly 10% to 90%, a gain of 70 percentage
points.

## Recent progress
Among young people the change is starker. More than 95% of those aged 15 to 24
now have basic literacy skills, meaning fewer than 15% cannot read a simple
sentence. Many countries that began the twentieth century with very low rates
have since gained 40 to 50 percentage points.
