# The rise of life expectancy

## A long climb
In 1900, the average life expectancy of a newborn was 32 years. By 2021 this
had more than tripled to 71 years. Two hundred years ago the global figure was
less than half of today's — around 45 years.

## Still deeply unequal
Progress has been uneven. In 2021 the global average was just over 70 years,
yet Nigeria's life expectancy was thirty years lower than Japan's. With Japan
close to 85 years, that puts Nigeria at around 65 years — still well below the
global average.
