# Vaccines and the lives they save

## Coverage today
Today, over 80% of the world's infants receive the full recommended course of
routine vaccines each year. That still leaves nearly one in five — around 40% —
without full protection.

## The lives saved
Researchers estimate that routine vaccination prevented about 154 million
deaths worldwide over the last fifty years — an average of roughly 8 million
lives saved every year, or more than 80,000 every single day.
