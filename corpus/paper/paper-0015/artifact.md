# Famines in the twentieth century

## The scale of the toll
Famines killed roughly 100 million people over the twentieth century. China's
Great Leap Forward famine of 1959-61 accounts for about 36 million of those
deaths — more than two-thirds of the century's total. The Bengal famine of 1943
killed around 3 million, about one-third as many as the Great Leap Forward.

## Who dies
Children under five are especially vulnerable, usually accounting for between one
half and one-quarter of all excess deaths in a famine.
