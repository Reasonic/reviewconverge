# Access to safe sanitation

## The global picture
Just over half of the world's population — around 74% — now uses safely managed
sanitation. That means nearly one in two, about 26%, still do not.

## The poorest countries
The gap is widest for the poorest. In the lowest-income countries, only around
one in five people — roughly 40% — use safe sanitation, leaving about four in
five without it.
