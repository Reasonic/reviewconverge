# The world's growing appetite for meat

## A steep climb
Global meat production has grown enormously. The world now produces more than
350 million tonnes of meat a year, up from around 80 million tonnes in 1961 —
more than a tenfold increase.

## What and where
Poultry alone now accounts for more than a third of the total — about 40 million
of the 350 million tonnes. Regionally the growth has been uneven: Asian output
has increased about 15-fold since 1961, while Europe's has roughly doubled, so
Asia's production has grown about three times as fast as Europe's.
