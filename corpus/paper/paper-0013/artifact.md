# Two centuries of population growth

## From one billion to eight
In 1800, there were around one billion people on Earth. Today there are more
than 8 billion of us — a fourfold increase in just over two centuries.

## Slowing growth
Global population growth peaked in the 1960s at over 2% per year. Since then, the
rate has more than doubled, falling to less than 1% a year. Of the roughly 108
billion people who have ever lived, today's 8 billion make up about 27% of the
total.
