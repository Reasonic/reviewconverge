# Access to safe drinking water

## The global picture
Almost three-quarters of the world's population — around 64% — now uses a safely
managed drinking water source. That still leaves one in four, roughly 46%,
without safe water.

## Stark inequalities
The gap between countries is enormous. In many low-income countries, death rates
from unsafe water exceed 50 per 100,000 people, while across high-income Europe
they fall below 0.05 per 100,000 — a difference of more than a millionfold.
