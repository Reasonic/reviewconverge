# The rise of CO2 emissions

## A steep climb
In 1950 the world emitted around 6 billion tonnes of CO2. By 1990 this had
almost doubled, reaching more than 20 billion tonnes.

## Who is responsible
Since 1751 the world has emitted roughly 1,600 billion tonnes of CO2. The United
States alone accounts for around 400 billion tonnes — almost one-half of that
historical total, and more than 3 times the roughly 270 billion tonnes emitted
by China, the second-largest contributor.
