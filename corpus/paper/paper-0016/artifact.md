# The rise of obesity

## A growing share
More than a third of adults in the United States were obese in 2016, meaning
fewer than one-quarter were not. Counting the overweight as well, over 60% of US
adults were overweight or obese, leaving over 40% at a healthy weight or below.

## A global problem
Worldwide, around two-fifths — about 20% — of adults were overweight or obese in
2016.
