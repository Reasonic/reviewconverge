# The world's forests

## How much forest remains
The world has around 4 billion hectares of forest. That is a little over
one-third of the planet's habitable land, and around one-half of all land
including deserts, ice sheets, and other uninhabitable ground.

## Where the forests are
Forests are unevenly distributed. Russia holds the largest share — over
800 million hectares, about two-fifths of the global total. Brazil is the only
other country with more than 10% of the world's forest cover — over 800 million
hectares. No other country comes close.
